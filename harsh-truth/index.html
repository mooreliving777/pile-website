OUR STORY

More than clothing.
A uniform for honesty.

The Harsh Truth Clothing Line LLC was created by Leonard E. Moore and Amanda Halterman to turn real experiences into bold, wearable statements. The brand is rooted in survival, accountability, culture, ambition and the belief that telling the truth can set a person free.

Every release is designed to start a conversation—and remind the person wearing it that their story still belongs to them.
